What Are Sister Sizes?

Sister sizes are bra sizes that hold the same cup volume even though the band and cup letter are different. For example, 34C, 32D, and 36B all have the same cup capacity. If your usual size is sold out or feels a bit off, a sister size can give you the same support with a different fit.

⬇️ Go Down a Band, Up a Cup

If the band feels too loose, try a smaller band and a larger cup. Example: 34C → 32D.

⬆️ Go Up a Band, Down a Cup

If the band feels too tight, try a larger band and a smaller cup. Example: 34C → 36B.

Why Use Sister Sizes?

  • Your size is out of stock – sister sizes give you more options.
  • Different brands fit differently – a sister size may fit better in a specific brand.
  • Your body changes – weight loss, gain, or pregnancy can change how your bra fits; sister sizing gives you flexibility.
  • Comfort – if your current bra digs in or rides up, a sister size might solve the problem.

What If I’m Between Sister Sizes?

If neither sister size feels quite right, you can also try going down a band and up two cups (e.g., 34C → 30E) or up a band and down two cups (e.g., 34C → 38A). This works if you need more or less volume while keeping the same band comfort.
