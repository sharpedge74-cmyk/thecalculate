# Editorial Policy – Our Commitment to Accuracy & Transparency

At **IMRango**, trust is our foundation. Every calculator, guide, and piece of content on this site is built on a commitment to **accuracy, transparency, and independence**.

We believe that when it comes to finding the right bra size, you deserve clear, honest information – not marketing fluff or hidden agendas. This editorial policy explains how we create, review, and update our content.

---

## 📏 Our Sizing Methodology

Every calculator on IMRango uses a **clearly disclosed method**:

| Method | Description | Used In |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| **Standard Band & Bust** | The industry-standard formula: underbust rounded to nearest even +4/+5 for band, bust difference for cup. | Bra Size, Cup Size, Band Size, Bust Size Calculators |
| **ABTF (6-Measurement)** | The community-developed "A Bra That Fits" method using loose, snug, and tight underbust plus standing, leaning, and lying bust. | ABTF Calculator |
| **Sister Size Logic** | Standard sister size mapping (band ±2, cup ∓1) for equivalent cup volume. | Sister Size Calculator |
| **Brand Conversions** | Brand-specific mappings based on official size charts from Victoria's Secret, Wacoal, ThirdLove, M&S, and Skims. | Brand Size Converter |
| **Volume Estimation** | Research-based volume tables mapping band and cup to approximate breast volume in cc/ml. | Breast Volume Calculator |

All formulas are **open and documented** – we don't hide how we calculate your size.

---

## 📅 Content Review & Updates

We take data accuracy seriously. Our content and sizing data are reviewed on a **regular schedule**:

- **Sizing formulas and charts:** Reviewed quarterly (every 3 months)
- **Brand mappings:** Reviewed when brands release updated size charts
- **Guides and articles:** Reviewed annually or when new research emerges
- **Last‑updated date:** Displayed on every calculator and guide page

If you notice an error or outdated information, please [contact us](/contact/) – we appreciate the heads-up.

---

## 🔍 Content Sources

Our content is built on a foundation of:

- **Peer-reviewed research** – anthropometric studies on breast sizing and body measurement
- **Industry standards** – official size charts from major bra brands and retailers
- **Community knowledge** – insights from the ABTF (A Bra That Fits) community and professional fitters
- **Independent verification** – cross-checking multiple sources to ensure consistency

We **do not** rely on AI-generated sizing data or unverified sources.

---

## 🧪 Editorial Independence

IMRango is an **independent measurement resource**. We are:

- **✅ Free from brand influence** – we don't accept payment or commissions from bra brands or retailers
- **✅ Free from advertising bias** – ads on our site are clearly marked and do not influence our content
- **✅ Free from affiliate marketing** – we don't earn commissions on product recommendations
- **✅ Free from sponsored content** – all guides and calculators are created by our team

We believe that **your size is personal** – and our advice should be too.

## 🔄 Corrections Policy

We strive for accuracy, but mistakes can happen. If we discover an error:

1. **We correct it promptly** – updates are made within 48 hours of discovery
2. **We note significant changes** – for major corrections, we add a "Last Updated" note
3. **We welcome feedback** – if you spot an error, please [let us know](/contact/)

We value your trust – and we work hard to keep it.
