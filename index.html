Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Victoria's Secret method work?

Victoria's Secret traditionally uses the +4 method – they add 4 inches to your underbust measurement to get the band size. This results in a looser band and smaller cup letter compared to modern snug fitting.

What is the ABTF method?

The ABTF (A Bra That Fits) method uses 6 measurements (loose, snug, tight underbust; standing, leaning, lying bust) for a more accurate fit, especially for larger busts and different shapes.

What's the difference between US and UK bra sizes?

US and UK bands are the same (e.g., 34 is 34 in both), but cups differ from DD up. US DDD = UK E, US G = UK F, US H = UK FF.

Are sister sizes the same cup volume?

Yes. Sister sizes hold the same cup volume but with different band and cup combinations. For example, 34C → 32D (tighter band) or 36B (looser band).

Does IMRango store my measurements?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser. We do not collect, store, or share any of your personal data or measurements.
