Understanding Your Bra Size: A Complete Guide

Finding the perfect bra starts with understanding your measurements. This bra size calculator is designed to give you the most accurate fit by combining multiple sizing methodologies. Whether you are looking for a bra measurement chart, need to determine bra cup size, or want to explore sister sizes, this tool covers all your needs. We support US, UK, EU, and AU sizing systems, ensuring you get the right conversion every time.

The Three Core Methods Explained

Most online tools rely on a single formula. Our bra size estimator goes further by offering three distinct engines, making it a true master bra size calculator.

  • Standard (2 measurements): Uses your snug underbust and standing bust. This is the quickest way to measure bra size and works well for many body types. This is often used by mainstream brands like Victoria's Secret and Wacoal.
  • ABTF (6 measurements): The A Bra That Fits (ABTF) calculator uses six data points (loose, snug, tight underbust; standing, leaning, lying bust) to average out discrepancies. This method is highly regarded in the bra-fitting community for delivering superior comfort, especially for larger busts.
  • Sister Size Converter: Once your base size is calculated, the tool automatically suggests sister sizes. For example, if you are a 34C, your sister sizes are 32D and 36B. This is essential when your exact size is out of stock or if you are between bands.

Regional Sizing: US vs UK vs EU vs AU

One of the biggest challenges is converting between regions. A bra size calculator US will give a different letter than a UK bra fitting calculator, even with the same bust-to-band difference. Our tool displays all four simultaneously.

  • US: Uses standard letters (AA, A, B, C, D, DD, DDD/F, G...).
  • UK: Uses (AA, A, B, C, D, DD, E, F, FF, G...). Note the difference after DD.
  • EU: Uses European cup progression (often A, B, C, D, E, F, G...) and band sizes like 65, 70, 75.
  • AU: Uses UK cup letters but band numbers are different (e.g., US 34 = AU 12).

If you are shopping internationally, use this bra measurement conversion feature to avoid expensive return shipping fees.

Brand-Specific Methods: Victoria's Secret, Wacoal, ThirdLove

Not all brands fit the same. We have included presets for popular retailers because they often use proprietary fitting techniques.

  • Victoria's Secret (+4): Historically, VS adds 4 inches to the underbust to determine the band. Selecting this option replicates the Victoria's Secret bra size calculator experience.
  • ThirdLove: Uses a slightly different rounding method for half-cup sizes.
  • M&S (Marks & Spencer): Uses the UK standard with specific band tension preferences.

How to Measure Your Bra Size at Home

You don't need a professional fitter to determine bust size. Here is how to use our bra size measurement chart effectively:

  1. Wear a non-padded bra: This ensures the tape sits naturally.
  2. Measure your underbust: Wrap the tape snugly around your ribcage, directly under the bust. Exhale normally.
  3. Measure your full bust: Wrap the tape around the fullest part of your chest, keeping it parallel to the floor.
  4. Input the values: Enter your numbers into the online bra size calculator above and get your result in seconds.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I figure out my bra cup size using this calculator?
Enter your underbust (snug) and full bust (standing) measurements. The tool subtracts your band size from your bust size. The difference (in inches) corresponds to a cup letter: 1 inch = A, 2 = B, 3 = C, 4 = D, 5 = DD, and so on. You can also switch to the ABTF (6-measurement) method for a more precise calculation.
What is the difference between a US and UK bra size calculator?
The US and UK systems differ mainly in cup progression. US sizes follow A, B, C, D, DD, DDD/F, G... while UK sizes use A, B, C, D, DD, E, F, FF, G... The band measurement is the same in inches, but the cup letters diverge after DD. This tool shows you your size in both systems side by side.
What are sister sizes in a bra size chart calculator?
Sister sizes are alternative bra sizes that hold the same breast volume. If your calculated size is 34C, your sister sizes are 32D (band down, cup up) and 36B (band up, cup down). They are useful if the calculated size is not available or doesn't fit your specific body shape.
How does the A Bra That Fits (ABTF) calculator differ from standard?
The standard calculator uses only two measurements: snug underbust and standing bust. The ABTF method uses six measurements: loose, snug, and tight underbust, plus standing, leaning, and lying bust. It averages the bust measurements and uses the snug underbust to determine the band, generally yielding a more accurate and comfortable fit, especially for softer breast tissue.
Can I measure my bra size at home without a professional fitter?
Yes. Use a flexible measuring tape. Wear a non-padded bra or no bra at all. Measure your underbust snugly around your ribcage, just below the bust. Then measure your bust at the fullest part. Enter these numbers into this bra size calculator to get an instant result.
What is the Victoria's Secret bra size method?
Victoria's Secret traditionally uses the "+4" method, where they add 4 inches to your underbust measurement to determine the band size. Our tool includes a 'Victoria's Secret' preset in the brand dropdown to replicate their in-store fitting algorithm.
How do I convert my bra size from US to EU?
EU bands are measured in centimeters (e.g., 70, 75, 80). Our tool automatically converts US band inches to the corresponding EU band size using the formula: (US band – 30) * 5 + 65. The cup letters are also mapped to the EU standard.
