How to Measure Your Bra Size at Home

Follow these four simple steps with a flexible cloth tape measure for an accurate fit.

Quick Answer & Sizing Formula Anthropometric fit methodology • Updated 2026

To calculate your true bra size: Band Size equals your snug ribcage underbust measurement rounded to the nearest even integer (e.g. 31″ → 32). Cup Size equals the difference between your full bust circumference and calculated band size, where each 1-inch difference corresponds to one cup letter (1″ = A, 2″ = B, 3″ = C, 4″ = D, 5″ = DD, 6″ = DDD/F).

1. Band Formula

Band = Even(Snug Underbust)

Modern ergonomic standard. Never add +4 inches; 85% of breast weight must be supported by the ribcage band.

2. Cup Formula

Cup = Full Bust – Band Size

1″ = A, 2″ = B, 3″ = C, 4″ = D, 5″ = DD, 6″ = DDD/F, 7″ = G, 8″ = H. For asymmetrical breasts, size to the fuller breast.

3. Sister Sizing Equivalence

Band ± 2 ↔ Cup ∓ 1

If band rides up, go down 1 band size and up 1 cup letter to preserve identical volume (e.g. 36B → 34C → 32D).

1

Snug Underbust

Stand straight, exhale naturally, and wrap the tape around your ribcage directly below your breasts. Keep it parallel to the floor and snug against skin.

2

Full Bust Line

Measure across the fullest projection of your breasts (usually across nipples). Do not pull tight; the tape should glide gently without compressing tissue.

3

Calculate Band

In modern sizing, your band equals your snug underbust rounded to the nearest even number. Avoid adding 4 inches, which ruins band support.

4

Determine Cup

Subtract your calculated band from your bust measurement. Each 1-inch difference represents one cup letter (1″ = A, 2″ = B, 3″ = C, 4″ = D, 5″ = DD).

Troubleshooting Common Bra Fit Issues

How to diagnose your current bra and discover exactly which adjustment to make.

Diagnosis 01

Band Rides Up Your Back

Cause: Your band is too loose. Shoulder straps take all the weight and pull the band upward between your shoulder blades.

Remedy: Sister size down one band size (e.g., from 36B to 34C). Fasten on the loosest hook when new.

Diagnosis 02

Shoulder Straps Dig in Deep

Cause: Straps are carrying the load the band should hold. 85% of breast weight should be anchored by the ribcage band, not your shoulders.

Remedy: A snugger, properly fitted band relieves shoulder strain immediately.

Diagnosis 03

Cup Spillage ("Quad-Boob")

Cause: Breast tissue bulges over the neckline or out toward the underarms. Your cups are at least 1–2 volumes too small.

Remedy: Keep your band size the same and move up 1 to 2 cup letters (e.g., 34C to 34D or 34DD).

Diagnosis 04

Underwire Pokes Breast Tissue

Cause: Underwire diameter is too narrow and sits directly on mammary glands rather than resting flat on your ribcage.

Remedy: Size up in cup volume or switch to an open-wire, wide-root bra style designed for your breast shape.

Diagnosis 05

Gapping at the Top of the Cup

Cause: Usually caused by a band that is too loose (cups tilt outward away from the body) or a cup shape mismatch (shallow breasts in a projected cup).

Remedy: Try a snugger band or a demi/balconette cut before assuming your cup size is too large.

Diagnosis 06

Floating Center Gore

Cause: The triangle between the cups doesn't press against your sternum. The cups lack enough depth to allow the wire to settle against your chest.

Remedy: Increase cup size by 1–2 letters until the center wire sits firmly against your breastbone.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, direct answers to common bra sizing questions.

Why does Victoria's Secret give me a 36B when I measure a 32DD?

Victoria's Secret uses the traditional +4 band method. If your underbust is 32 inches, they add 4 inches to assign you a 36 band. Because a 36 band is much wider, the cup volume must shrink to keep overall capacity similar, putting you into a B cup. In reality, a 36 band will ride up your back and fail to provide support. A 32DD provides true ribcage anchorage and proper cup depth.

What is sister sizing and when should I use it?

Sister sizes are pairs of bra sizes that share identical cup volume. If you wear a 34C, its sister sizes are 32D (tighter band) and 36B (looser band). Use sister sizing when a particular bra style runs tight or loose in the band, or when your exact size is out of stock.

Why do UK and US cup letters diverge after D?

US sizing uses: D, DD, DDD/F, G, H, I, J, K. UK sizing uses double letters for specific increments: D, DD, E, F, FF, G, GG, H, HH, J, JJ. Therefore, a US 34DDD is equivalent to a UK 34E, while a US 34H is a UK 34FF. Many high-support bra brands (Freya, Panache, Elomi) use UK sizing.

Should I measure over a bra or with bare breasts?

For your underbust, measure directly against bare skin. For your bust, you can measure braless or wearing a thin, unpadded, non-push-up bra. If your breast tissue is soft or pendulous, the leaning 90° measurement in our ABTF mode gives the most accurate representation of breast volume without needing a bra.

Which hook should a new bra fasten on?

A new bra should fit snugly on the loosest hook. Over months of wear and washing, the elastane in the band naturally relaxes. As the band stretches out, you gradually move to the middle and tightest hooks to maintain optimal support.
