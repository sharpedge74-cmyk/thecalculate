The Ultimate Bra Size Sizing & Measurement Guide

Finding the right bra size is one of the most essential aspects of everyday comfort, posture support, and body confidence. However, over 80% of women are currently wearing the wrong bra size without realizing it. Whether you are using a bra size calculator, exploring a victoria secret bra size calculator, or figuring out your sister sizes, this guide provides complete, accurate information for all breast shapes and brand fitting formulas.

Key Takeaway: Your bra size is not a permanent label. It changes with weight fluctuations, hormonal shifts, exercise, and age. Measuring yourself every 6 months using our bra measurement calculator ensures you always shop with confidence.

How to Measure Your Bra Size at Home: Step-by-Step

To measure bra size at home correctly, all you need is a flexible measuring tape and a mirror. Follow these exact steps:

  1. Step 1: Take your Underbust Measurement (Band Size)
    Wrap the measuring tape directly beneath your bust around your ribcage. Pull it snug—it should lie flat against your skin without digging in. Ensure the tape is straight across your back. Read the measurement in inches or centimeters.
  2. Step 2: Take your Bust Measurement (Cup Size)
    Wrap the measuring tape loosely around the fullest part of your chest (usually across the center of your nipples). Do not squeeze your breast tissue. Keep your arms down at your sides and exhale naturally.
  3. Step 3: Calculate the Difference
    Subtract your underbust/band size from your full bust measurement. Each 1-inch difference corresponds to one cup size step (1" = A, 2" = B, 3" = C, 4" = D, 5" = DD, etc.).

Victoria's Secret Bra Size Calculator Method vs. Modern Sizing

When shopping at popular retailers like Victoria's Secret, Wacoal, M&S (Marks & Spencer), ThirdLove, or Skims, you might notice that sizing recommendations vary. Retailers like Victoria's Secret historically utilize the +4 bra measurement method, where 4 inches are added to an even underbust measurement (or 5 to an odd measurement) to calculate the band size.

For example, if your snug underbust is 30 inches and your bust is 36 inches:

  • Modern Snug Band Sizing: Band = 30, Bust = 36. Difference = 6 inches. Size = 30DDD/E (US) / 30F (UK).
  • Victoria's Secret +4 Method: Band = 30 + 4 = 34. Bust = 36. Difference = 2 inches. Size = 34B.

What are Sister Sizes and How Do They Work?

Sister sizes are bra sizes that hold the exact same volume of breast tissue despite having different band and cup letter combinations. If a bra fits perfectly in the cup but feels too tight or loose in the ribband, you can switch to a sister size:

  • Sister Size Down (Tighter Band): Decrease the band by 1 size (e.g. 34 to 32) and increase the cup by 1 letter (e.g. C to D). Result: 32D has the same cup volume as 34C.
  • Sister Size Up (Looser Band): Increase the band by 1 size (e.g. 34 to 36) and decrease the cup by 1 letter (e.g. C to B). Result: 36B has the same cup volume as 34C.

Specialized Fitting: Sports Bras, Strapless & Plus Size Bras

When shopping for specialized garments, fitting considerations change:

  • Sports Bra Size Calculator: High-impact sports bras require a firmer band to prevent bounce. Stick to your true snug underbust band size rather than the +4 method.
  • Strapless Bra Fitting: Since there are no shoulder straps to lift weight, 100% of the support comes from the band. You may need to select a sister size with a tighter band for extra security.
  • Plus Size & Maternity Bras: For larger band sizes or nursing mothers, flexible wide bands and stretchy cups ensure comfortable support through daily body shifts.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I figure out my bra cup size accurately?

Enter your snug underbust and standing bust measurements into our online bra cup size calculator. The difference in inches determines your cup letter: 1" = A cup, 2" = B cup, 3" = C cup, 4" = D cup, 5" = DD cup, and so on.

What is the difference between US and UK bra size calculators?

US and UK band sizes use identical inch numbers (e.g. 32, 34, 36). However, UK cup sizing includes double letters such as E, FF, GG, HH, whereas US sizing uses D, DD, DDD/F, G, H, I, J. Our tool displays both simultaneously.

Can I convert bra size from inches to centimeters (cm)?

Yes. Toggle the Unit selector at the top of the calculator to Centimeters. Underbust measurements in cm map to European (EU) band sizes (e.g., 75 cm = EU 75 band).

Why is my calculated Victoria's Secret bra size different from ABTF?

Victoria's Secret traditionally uses the +4 method which results in a looser band and smaller cup letter, whereas the ABTF (A Bra That Fits) method calculates a snugger band with a larger cup letter for maximum support.
