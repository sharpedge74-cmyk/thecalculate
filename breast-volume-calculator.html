What Is Breast Volume?

Breast volume is the total amount of breast tissue in cubic centimeters (cc) or milliliters (ml). One cubic centimeter equals one milliliter. This measurement is used in plastic surgery for breast augmentation and reduction, as well as for custom bra fitting and prosthetics.

Important: This calculator provides an estimate based on bra size. Actual volume varies by breast shape, density, and individual anatomy. Always consult a medical professional for medical decisions.

How Breast Volume Is Estimated

This calculator uses established estimation methods based on bra size. The volume is estimated using:

  • Band size – determines the chest circumference.
  • Cup letter – determines the breast projection.
  • Volume tables – based on anthropometric studies of breast size and volume.

📊 Volume Ranges by Cup Size

  • AA: 100 – 150 cc
  • A: 150 – 250 cc
  • B: 250 – 350 cc
  • C: 350 – 450 cc
  • D: 450 – 550 cc
  • DD: 550 – 700 cc
  • DDD/F: 700 – 850 cc
  • G: 850 – 1000 cc
  • H: 1000 – 1200 cc
  • I+: 1200+ cc

📐 Band Size Impact

The same cup letter can have different volumes at different band sizes. A 34C has a smaller volume than a 38C because the circumference is larger.

Example: 32D ≈ 34C ≈ 36B (sister sizes – same volume, different band).

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is this breast volume calculator?

This is an estimate based on bra size. Actual volume varies by breast shape, density, and individual anatomy. For medical decisions, always consult a professional.

What's the difference between cc and ml?

One cubic centimeter (cc) equals one milliliter (ml). They are the same measurement – interchangeable in most contexts.

Can I use this for implant sizing?

This tool provides a general estimate. Implant sizing depends on individual anatomy, desired outcome, and surgeon recommendations. Always consult a board-certified plastic surgeon.
