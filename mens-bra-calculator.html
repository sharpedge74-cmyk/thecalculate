Bra Sizing for Everyone

Bra sizing isn't just for one body type. Many people wear bras for different reasons:

  • Gynecomastia: Enlarged breast tissue in men, causing discomfort or self-consciousness. A supportive bra can provide comfort and shape.
  • Transgender Women (MTF): Hormone therapy may cause breast development. A well-fitting bra offers comfort and affirmation.
  • Non-Binary / Gender Diverse: Anyone seeking chest support or gender-affirming undergarments.
  • Men with broader chests: Some men find compression or support bras helpful for sports, back support, or personal comfort.
💜 Remember: There is no "right" or "wrong" reason to wear a bra. Your comfort and confidence matter. This calculator is here to help you find a size that works for you.

How to Measure for an Inclusive Bra

Measuring for a bra when you have a different chest shape is very similar to standard measuring – with a few considerations:

  1. Underbust / Chest: Wrap the tape snugly around your chest just below the bust area (or where you'd want the band to sit). Men and broader chests may need to size up in the band – our calculator adjusts for that.
  2. Full Chest: Measure around the fullest part of your chest – typically across the nipples or the widest point. Keep the tape level and don't compress tissue.
  3. Enter your numbers: Input them into the calculator above. We'll suggest a size that balances comfort and support.

Style Recommendations

👕 Compression Bras

Flatten the chest – good for gynecomastia or those who prefer a flatter look. Often wire-free and available in neutral colours.

🩱 Soft Bralettes

Gentle support without underwire. Great for everyday comfort and light support. Many brands offer inclusive sizing.

🏃 Sports Bras

Designed for movement – excellent for active individuals. High-impact styles offer maximum support.

💗 Gender-Affirming Bras

Lace, satin, or cotton – choose what feels affirming. Many brands now offer gender-inclusive collections.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it normal for men to wear bras?

Yes! Many men wear bras for gynecomastia, support, or personal comfort. There's no shame in wearing what makes you feel comfortable and confident.

What if I don't want a feminine style?

Many brands offer compression and sports bras in neutral colours and simple designs. You can also find gender-neutral options that focus on function over fashion.

My chest is broad – will a bra fit?

Many brands now offer larger band sizes (up to 56+) and cups designed for broader chests. Our calculator starts at 38 band and goes up to 56, with cup sizes A–DD for inclusive fitting.

Does IMRango store my measurements?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser. We do not collect, store, or share any of your personal data or measurements. Your privacy matters.
