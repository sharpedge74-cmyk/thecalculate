What Is Plus Size Bra Fitting?

Plus size bras are designed for fuller figures with wider bands (38–56) and larger cups (DD–N). Unlike standard bras, plus size bras often have:

  • Wider, cushioned straps – to prevent digging and distribute weight.
  • Three or more hook rows – for extra band support and adjustability.
  • Fuller coverage cups – to encapsulate breast tissue without spillage.
  • Reinforced side panels – for better shaping and support.
Key tip: In plus size bras, the band does 80% of the work. A properly fitted band should feel firm but not painful – you should be able to fit two fingers under it comfortably.

How to Measure for a Plus Size Bra

📏 1. Underbust (Band)

Wrap the tape snugly around your ribcage, just under your bust. Take the measurement after you exhale. Round to the nearest even number for your band size.

Example: 37" → 38 band

📐 2. Bust (Cup)

Measure around the fullest part of your bust – usually across the nipples. Keep the tape level and don't pull it tight. This gives you your cup letter.

Example: 46" bust – 38" band = 8" difference → H cup

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between plus size and regular bras?

Plus size bras typically start at a 38 band and go up to 56, with cup sizes from DD to N. They feature wider straps, more hook rows, and fuller coverage cups for better support and comfort.

Should I size up or down in plus size bras?

Start with your calculated size, then try sister sizes. Plus size bands should fit firmly – if you can pull the band more than 2 inches away from your body, go down a band size.

What brands specialize in plus size bras?

Popular plus size bra brands include Elomi, Goddess, Panache Sculptresse, Glamorise, and Cacique (Lane Bryant). Many offer bands up to 56 and cups up to N.
