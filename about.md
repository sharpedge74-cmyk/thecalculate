# About IMRango

**IMRango** is a free, independent measurement resource dedicated to helping you find the perfect bra fit – without the guesswork, without the pressure, and without sharing your data.

We believe that **every body deserves a comfortable, confident fit**. Whether you're shopping for your first bra, looking for a sports bra, navigating maternity sizing, or seeking a more accurate size through the ABTF method, our calculators are here to support you.

## What We Offer

- ✅ **14 free calculators** – from standard band/cup to ABTF, sister sizing, brand conversion, plus size, maternity, and more.
- ✅ **US, UK, EU, AU, and FR sizing** – compare international sizes instantly.
- ✅ **Privacy-first** – all calculations run in your browser. We never store or share your measurements.
- ✅ **Regularly updated** – our sizing data and guides are reviewed to stay accurate.
- ✅ **Inclusive sizing** – tools for men, transgender women, non-binary individuals, and all body types.

## Why Trust IMRango?

Every calculator on this site runs on a **clearly disclosed method** – the standard band-and-bust formula, the community ABTF three-measurement approach, or direct sister-size logic. You always know what's behind your result, not just a number.

Our sizing data and guides are reviewed on a regular schedule, with a visible **last-updated date** on every page. Results here are **estimates** meant to make shopping easier – not a substitute for a professional fitting.

We are an independent resource. **We don't sell bras, take commissions, or promote specific brands.** Our only goal is to give you accurate information.

## Meet the Author

**Sarees Ali** is the founder of IMRango. With a background in data analysis and a passion for body-positive design, Sarees created IMRango to make bra sizing simple, accessible, and private for everyone.

> *"I've seen too many people struggle with uncomfortable bras because they didn't have accurate sizing information. I built IMRango to change that – one measurement at a time."*
